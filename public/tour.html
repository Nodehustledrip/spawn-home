    Ship

    Go Live opens a shareable public HTTPS URL from your machine (Ship → Open to the internet). Attach your domain, load secrets and plugins — without leaving the builder.

    • Private until you press Go Live
    • Public HTTPS from your machine — copy or QR
    • Custom domain: DNS + port-forward for lasting traffic
    • Iterate after — Build chat still works
