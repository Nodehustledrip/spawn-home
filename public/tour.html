Product tour

From idea to live URL

Three moves inside Spawn: build the app, run it locally, ship a public link. No cloud sandbox. Real files on your machine.

  1. 01

    Build

    Describe what you want. Spawn scaffolds a real project and opens Build chat — ask for a change and it edits the file, then refreshes the preview.

    You’re looking at structure and diffs, not a throwaway demo.
