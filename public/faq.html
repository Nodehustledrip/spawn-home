Basics

What is Spawn?

A private app builder on your machine. Create a real app, run a localhost preview, change it in Build chat, then Go Live for a public HTTPS URL — with domains, secrets, and plugins in the same place.

How is this different from chat-only builders?

Spawn edits real project files, shows the preview on your machine, and ships a public URL with domains and secrets included. It’s a builder loop — create → run → build → live — not a one-shot chat that dumps disposable code. See Why Spawn.

What do I need to get started?

Just a browser — Build is free, no account. When you’re ready, publish to Spawn Cloud for a live <name>.spawnapp.org URL that stays up 24/7. Describe an app or open existing files, hit Run for a localhost preview, then iterate in chat. No separate deploy dance just to try a change. Walkthrough: How it works.

Privacy & machine

Is Spawn local or cloud?

Building stays local. Project files, preview, and secrets live on your machine until you choose to publish. Go Live puts a public site on the internet; the builder is not a browser-only sandbox that holds your code.

Where do my code and secrets live?

Code stays on your machine until you publish. Secrets (API keys, env vars) live in Spawn’s Secrets and inject at run and deploy — not in your source tree. This site’s waitlist email handling is on Privacy.

Shipping

What happens when I Go Live?

Publish to Spawn Cloud from the desktop app and your project goes live at https://<name>.spawnapp.org — copy the link or scan the QR. Same project you ran on localhost, now up 24/7 even with your PC off. Keep iterating and republish to ship changes.

Can I use a custom domain?

Yes. Every published app lives at https://<name>.spawnapp.org, and you can connect a domain you own from Spawn’s Domains screen or spawnapp.org/cloud/domains. Buy the domain at any registrar, set the two nameservers Spawn shows, and it goes live with HTTPS automatically — root and www together, with your existing email records carried over. Custom domains are part of Spawn Pro; every free published app keeps its <name>.spawnapp.org address.

Does my computer need to stay on?

No. Apps published to Spawn Cloud are served from the cloud at https://<name>.spawnapp.org and stay up 24/7 with your PC off. Your source and secrets still live on your machine.

Access

Do I need an invite?

No. Spawn is free to use today at /build. Pro plans are on the pricing page.

How much does Spawn cost?

Free covers templates, live preview, chat edits and zip download — 2 builds and 20 edits a day with no account, or 5 saved builds and 30 edits a day with a free account (email link, no password). Free accounts can publish to Spawn Cloud at yourname.spawnapp.org with a small “Made with Spawn” badge. Pro is $12/month or $120/year: connect your own domain, no badge, 25 saved builds with undo history, 300 edits a day, and priority email support.
