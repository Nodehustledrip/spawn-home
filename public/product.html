Builder surfaces

Eight capabilities in one product — feature-forward, not a walkthrough. Jump any surface; Tour and How cover the loop.

  • New project Create
    Create

    Start a real app

    Describe what you’re building or open files already on disk. Spawn scaffolds a project on your machine — not a throwaway sandbox.

  • localhost:5173 Running
    Run

    Local preview

    Hit Run and see the app live on localhost. Same process you’ll ship — no deploy required to try a change.

  • Build · chat Diff
    Build chat

    Change it in English

    Ask for a pricing table, auth flow, or layout fix. Spawn edits the files, shows the diff, and refreshes the preview — with your keys, on your project.

  • Go Live Live
    Go Live

    Public URL

    Open to the internet for a copyable HTTPS link (and QR) from your machine. Share it, keep iterating — without switching tools to ship.

  • Domains Connected
    Domains

    Your hostname

    Attach a custom domain from inside Spawn. Lasting traffic needs DNS to your WAN IP plus router port-forward — Spawn shows the steps.

  • Secrets Env
    Secrets

    Keys stay put

    Store API keys and env vars in Secrets. They’re injected at run and deploy time — they don’t live in your source tree.

  • Plugins Auth · DB
    Plugins

    Extend without leaving

    Wire auth, storage, email, and more from inside the builder. Production pieces stay in one place — not a pile of side consoles.

  • Account You
    Account

    Your seat, your machine

    Sign in, manage invites, and keep builder settings with you. Private beta seats land here first — honest invite waves, no fake wait counts.
