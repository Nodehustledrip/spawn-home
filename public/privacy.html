Privacy

Spawn stores emails and contact details you submit for early access, leads, or accounts on your host. When authentication is enabled, we use an HttpOnly session cookie.

We do not sell your data. Access requests submitted on this site may be stored locally in your browser until a server endpoint is connected.

Apps you build with Spawn stay on your machine until you choose to publish. Secrets and domain settings you configure in Spawn are yours.
