How it works

From idea to a live URL — without leaving Spawn.

Spawn is a private app builder that runs on your machine. You create a real app, see it run, change it by chatting, then publish a public link.

  1. Create
    Describe your app…
    1

    Create your app

    Start from a blank workspace or your own files. Tell Spawn what you’re building — a landing page, a dashboard, a billing flow — and it scaffolds a real project on your machine.

  2. localhost:5173
    2

    Run a preview

    Hit Run and see your app live in a local preview. No deploy required to try it — you’re looking at the real thing running on your machine.

  3. Build chat
    Add a pricing table with three tiers
    Updated Pricing — Free, Pro, Team. Preview refreshed.
    3

    Change it in chat

    Open Build and ask for a change in plain English. Spawn edits the files, shows you what changed, and refreshes the preview. No need to hunt through code first.

  4. Go Live
    Public URL https://your-app.spawnapp.org
    4

    Publish a public URL

    Go Live puts your app on the internet. Custom domains, secrets, and plugins stay inside Spawn — you don’t leave the builder to ship.

What you get

  • A private builder Runs on your machine — your code and secrets don’t leave until you publish.
  • A live site or app A real public URL with HTTPS, not a temporary preview link.
  • Domains, secrets, plugins Wire your domain, API keys, and plugins without leaving Spawn.
