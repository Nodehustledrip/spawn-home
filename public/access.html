What beta actually is

Honest expectations — so you know what you’re joining.

  • On your machine Spawn runs locally. Your files, preview, and secrets stay on your computer until you choose to publish.
  • Create → change → ship Describe an app, chat to edit, preview on localhost, then Go Live for a public HTTPS URL you can share.
  • Ship kit inside Domains, secrets, and plugins live in the same product — not a throwaway prototype toy.
  • Still evolving Private beta means edges get sharper with early feedback. We build in public on spawnapp.org — no vapor brochure.
