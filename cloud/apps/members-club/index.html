    Questions

    How do I get access after paying?

    Create your account, then pay on Stripe’s secure checkout with the same email. The club switches your access on once your payment is confirmed. You can then sign in and open the library.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, at any time. Your membership is billed by Stripe, and the club can cancel it for you on request.

    Do I need special equipment?

    A jar, a bowl, a kitchen scale and a lidded oven-safe pot. Lesson one has the full list.

    The Crumb Club

    The Crumb Club is a fictional business. This is a demo membership site made with Spawn. The lessons are real baking guidance, but the people, prices and club are illustrative.
