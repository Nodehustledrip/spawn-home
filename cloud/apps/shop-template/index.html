    The studio

    Made slowly, in small runs.

    Each piece is thrown, trimmed and glazed by hand, so no two are quite the same. We fire in small kilns, a few dozen pieces at a time.

    Everything is food-safe stoneware. Dishwasher and microwave safe unless the product page says otherwise.

    Shipping & returns

    How much is shipping?

    Flat $8 in the US, free on orders over $75. Orders ship in 3–5 working days, wrapped in recycled paper.

    How does checkout work?

    Your cart is saved in this browser. When you check out, the shop gets your order and you pay on Stripe’s secure checkout page.

    Can I return something?

    Unused pieces can be returned within 30 days. Get in touch with your order reference.
