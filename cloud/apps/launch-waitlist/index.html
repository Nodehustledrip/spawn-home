Questions

When does it launch?

The countdown shows the date we’re aiming for. Everyone on the list hears first, and invites go out in waves.

How do referrals work?

After you join you get a personal link. When someone signs up through it, they’re credited to you. We use that to decide the order of early invites — no points, no prizes, just earlier access.

Will it cost anything?

Early access is free. We’ll share pricing before launch, well before anything is charged.

What will you do with my email?

Only use it to tell you about Pocketbook. We don’t sell or share your address.
